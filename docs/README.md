## Important notes

- These are templates for a LATAM Spanish-language affiliate review site, not legal advice for a specific country. Mexico, Colombia, Brazil, Argentina, Chile and Peru have different consumer-protection and data-protection laws.
- Have a local lawyer review them before you scale, especially the Privacy Policy and Health Disclaimer.
- Do not copy another site's legal pages verbatim — these are drafted for nutritionalreviews.org specifically.
- Date on all pages: 27 October 2026. Update the date whenever you edit them.
